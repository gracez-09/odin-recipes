jollof rice

Description

Nigerian jollof rice is a very popular dish in nigeria and is enjoyed on many different occasions like wedding parties,family gatherings, birthday parties and even as an everyday dish.it can be server as a main dish oran along side dish.This recipe contains easy to follow steps for preparing the delicious jollof rice from start to finish.ggggg

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of chopped onions
  • 2 red bell pepper seeded and chopped
  • 3 scotch bonnet pepper
  • fresh tomatoes
  • 1 cup of vegetable oil
  • 1/4 cup of tomato paste
  • 4 teaspoons of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 teaspoons of garlic powder
  • 2 teaspoons of ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon of onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon of ground white pepper
  • 1 teaspoon of smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon powder or chicken base
  • salt to taste
  • 4 cups of parboiled rice,rinsed until the water runs clear
  • 5 cups warmed chicken broth

Steps

  1. combine 1/2 of the chopped onions,bell pepper,scotch bonnet pepper and tomatoes in a blender.Blend until completely smooth.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot over medium heat.Add remaining onions and cook until soften and lightly golden,6 to 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in tomato paste and cook,stirring frequently,until it darkens from bright red to deep brick-red color,8 to 10 minutes.
  4. pour in blend tomato mixture.Cook stirring occasionally,until most of the liquid has evarporated and the sauce thickens,20 to 25 minutes.The oil should begin separating at thr edges.
  5. season with curry powder,garlic powder,onion powder,ground ginger,white pepper,smoked paparika,chicken bouillon and salt.Stir to combine.Cook for 2 minutes.
  6. Add rinsed rice and gently until every grain is evenly coated with tomato mixture.pour in warm chicken broth and stir once.Bring to a gentle boil
  7. Reduce heat to low.Cover the pot tightly with aluminium foil and then the lid to trap the steam.Cook without stirring for 30 to 35 minutes.
  8. Carefully remove the lid and foil.Gently fluff the rice.if it is not yet tender,sprinkle a few tablespoons of warm water around the edges of the pot,cover again,and cook for another 10 minutes.
  9. For authentic party-style flavor,leave the rice over very low heat for the final 5 to 10 minutes without stirring.This allows the bottom layer to light caramelize,creating the signature smoky flavor that makes Nigerian party jollof so memorable.
  10. Remove the bay leaves,gently fluff the rice once more.Serve hot
