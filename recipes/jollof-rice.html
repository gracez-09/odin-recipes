jollof rice

Description

Nigerian jollof rice is a very popular dish in nigeria and is enjoyed on many different occasions like wedding parties,family gatherings, birthday parties and even as an everyday dish.it can be server as a main dish oran along side dish.This recipe contains easy to follow steps for preparing the delicious jollof rice from start to finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of chopped onions
  • 2 red bell pepper seeded and chopped
  • 3 scotch bonnet pepper
  • fresh tomatoes
  • 1 cup of vegetable oil
  • 1/4 cup of tomato paste
  • 4 teaspoons of curry powder
  • 2 teaspoons of dry thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 teaspoons of garlic powder
  • 2 teaspoons of ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon of onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon of ground white pepper
  • 1 teaspoon of smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon powder or chicken base
  • salt to taste
  • 4 cups of parboiled rice,rinsed until the water runs clear
  • 5 cups warmed chicken broth
