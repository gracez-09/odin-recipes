spaghetti

Description

Spaghetti is a delicios and versatile meal that can be prepared with a variety of ingredients,sauces,and seasoning.it is simple to make and can be enjoyed for lunch,dinner,or as a quick and satisfying meal.The following guide provides simple steps to help you prepare a delicious plate of spaghetti at home.

Ingredients

  • 4 italian sausage links
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 yellow onion,chopped
  • 2 table spoon of olive oil
  • 1(15 ounce) can tomato sauce>
  • 1(14.5 ounce) can whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh oregano
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon of dried basil
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1(16 ounce) packaged dry spaghetti
  • 1/2cup grated parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. slice sausages lenghtwise,leaving links attached along one side;lay flat in a large skillet.Cook over medium heat until browned and cooked through,5 minutes, flipping once.Transfer to plate;set aside.
  3. Add ground beef,onions,garlic and olive oil to the ssame skillet over medium heat cook and stir until onion is transluscent and beef is browned and crumbly,about 10 minutes.Drain all but 2 tablespoons drippings from thhe skillet.
  4. Stir in tomatoes,oregano,salt,basil,bay leaf,and black pepper;simmer uncovered over low heat until flavors have blended,about 1 hour,stirring occasionally.Remove and discard bay leaf.
  5. preheat the oven to 350 degrees (f),175 degree (c).Bring a pot of lightly salted water to a boil.Cook spaghetti in the boiling water,stirring occasionally,until tender yet firm to the bite,8 to 10 minutes.Drain.
  6. Stir spaghetti into sauce.
  7. Divide spaghetti and sauce among iindividual oven-safe baking dishes or plates;top each with a cooked half sausage ans sprinkle with parmesan cheese.
  8. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and begins to brown,5 to 10 minutes.
