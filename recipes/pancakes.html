Pancakes

Description

pancakes are popular dish made from a simple batter of flour and other ingredients.they are soft,fluffy and delicious,it is usually served with toppings such as syrup honey,fresh fruits,or butter.pancakes are easy to prepare and are perfect for breakfastbrunch or as a quick and tasty snack.below are easy to follow guide on how to make delicious fluffy pancakes at home

Ingredients

  • flour:1 cup all-purpose flour
  • sugar:2 tablespoons of white sugar
  • Baking powder:2 tablespoons
  • salt:1/2 teaspoons or to taste
  • milk:1 cup of milk
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • eggs:1 large egg
